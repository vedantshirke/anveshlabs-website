About

The name means a quest.

Anvesh, from Sanskrit, means a search, a discovery. Every product we build starts as a search for what would genuinely help.

We build for people who are climbing: the aspirant studying in a library after a full day's work, the teacher handling forty students with a register and a pen.

Our mark shows that climb. Five dots rising from deep ink into saffron, because real progress happens one small step at a time.

“Where you start doesn't decide where you end up. Steady practice does.”
