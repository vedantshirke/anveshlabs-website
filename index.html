02The name

अन्वेष

anveshSanskrit · noun
  1. A quest; a search.
  2. The act of discovering something that was there all along.

Every product is a search.

We start by looking closely: in study libraries, staff rooms and WhatsApp groups. We build only once we've found something worth fixing.

Rising, step by step.

Our mark is five dots climbing from deep ink into saffron. It's how progress really happens: one small step a day.

Rooted here.

We build in Marathi first, for Maharashtra first. Being specific is how we make things that feel made for you.

03Craft

Small studio. Serious craft.

We design, build and run our products end to end, from the first screen to the database behind it.

i.

Mobile apps that feel native

Fast, polished apps for Android and iOS, built with Flutter, React Native and Expo.

ii.

Backends built to scale

Secure, multi-tenant systems on Supabase and PostgreSQL, with row-level security from day one.

iii.

Marathi-first design

Devanagari typography, bilingual interfaces and copy that sounds like a person, not a translation.

iv.

Made for budget phones

Light downloads, quick loading, and offline modes that keep working on patchy networks.

v.

AI, checked by people

We use AI to move faster, and real experts review what it produces. Accuracy comes first.

vi.

Habits that stick

Streaks, leagues and gentle nudges, designed around a real goal instead of empty badges.
