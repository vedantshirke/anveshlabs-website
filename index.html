Our story

Five names. One quest.

अन्वेष (anvesh) means a quest, a search, a discovery. It's also our family: each letter stands for one of us.

AOne family
NFive people
VOne letter each
ERising together
SHInto one word

We're two brothers from Maharashtra. We didn't start with many advantages. What we had was each other, and a habit of working things out the hard way.

That's why we build for people who are climbing, too: the aspirant studying in a library after a full day's work, the teacher handling forty students with a register and a pen. We know what it means to want more and to have to earn every step.

Our mark shows that climb. Five dots, one for each of us, rising from deep ink into saffron.

“Where you start doesn't decide where you end up. Steady practice does.” सुरुवात कुठून झाली यापेक्षा, रोजचा सराव महत्त्वाचा.
