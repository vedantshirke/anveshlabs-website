Delete your Sarav account

You can delete your account and personal data at any time, with or without the app installed.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Sarav and tap Profile.
  2. Tap Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and your data is erased within 30 days.

Option 2: Request it here, without the app

  1. Email hello@anveshlabs.com with the subject “Delete my Sarav account”.
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your account, or include the phone number you signed up with.
  3. We'll confirm it's you, then delete your account within 7 days and send you a confirmation.

Email a deletion request

If the button doesn't open your email app, write to hello@anveshlabs.com yourself.

Have Sarav Pro? Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in the Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Sarav → Cancel.

What we delete

  • Your account details: name, display name, phone number or email
  • Your preparation profile: exams, district, exam date and language
  • Your practice history, scores, streaks, mistakes deck and league history
  • Your notification token and support messages

What we keep, and for how long

  • Purchase records (date, product and amount, never card or UPI details) for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Encrypted backups containing your data are overwritten within 90 days of deletion.
  • Combined statistics that can't identify you, such as how many people answered a question correctly.

Just want a fresh start?

You don't need to delete your account to start over. In Profile → Settings → Reset progress you can clear your practice history and streak while keeping your account.

Questions? Write to hello@anveshlabs.com or read our Privacy Policy.
