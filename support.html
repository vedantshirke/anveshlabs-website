Frequently asked questions

Subscriptions

How do I cancel Sarav Pro?

Open the Google Play Store → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Sarav → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing.

Make sure you're signed in to the same Google account you paid with, then go to Profile → Settings → Restore purchases. If it still doesn't appear, email us your Google Play order number (it starts with “GPA.”).

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. You can usually request one within 48 hours of purchase at play.google.com. If you've been charged by mistake, write to us too and we'll help.

What's the difference between Pro and an exam pass?

Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription that renews automatically. An exam pass is a one-time payment that unlocks Pro until the end date shown when you buy it, usually the end of your exam cycle, and it doesn't renew.

Practice and leagues

I found a wrong answer.

Tap Report question below the explanation and tell us what's wrong. Every report is checked by a person, and we aim to fix confirmed errors within 24 hours. Thank you, because this keeps Sarav accurate for everyone.

How do I change my exam or district?

Go to Profile → My exam. If you change your district, you'll join that district's league from the next weekly round.

I lost my streak. Can I get it back?

Streak freezes protect your streak on days you miss. Pro users get them automatically, and free users can earn one by watching a short ad. If you lost a streak because of an app problem, email us and we'll look into it.

How do leagues work?

Each week you compete with aspirants preparing for the same exam in your district. The top players move up a level when the week ends. Other players see only your display name, district and score.

I'm not getting my daily reminder.

Check that notifications for Sarav are allowed in your phone's Settings → Apps → Sarav → Notifications, and that battery saver isn't blocking the app. Some phones (Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo, Realme) also need Autostart turned on.

Account and privacy

Is Sarav an official MPSC or Maharashtra Police app?

No. Sarav is an independent practice app made by Anvesh Labs. It is not affiliated with MPSC, Maharashtra Police or any government body. Always check official websites for notifications, dates and results.

How do I delete my account?

In Sarav, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account, or request deletion from our website.

What data does Sarav collect?

Only what it needs to work: your account details, exam and district, and your practice progress. We never sell your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
