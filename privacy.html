Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Applies to the Sarav app and anveshlabs.com

The short version

  • We collect only what Sarav needs to work: your account details, your exam and district, and your practice progress.
  • We never sell your personal data.
  • Payments go through Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob. Sarav Pro removes them.
  • You can see, correct or delete your data at any time. Delete your account here.

1. Who we are

Anvesh Labs (“we”, “us”) builds and operates the Sarav app and the website anveshlabs.com. Anvesh Labs is operated by [LEGAL NAME OF PROPRIETOR / ENTITY], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], Maharashtra, India. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here.

Contact: hello@anveshlabs.com

This policy covers Sarav and this website. Our other apps, such as Deskmate, will be added here before they launch.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name or display name, and your [phone number / email address / Google sign-in, as used by the app].
  • Your preparation profile: the exams you're preparing for, your district, your exam date and your preferred language.
  • Support and feedback: messages you send us and questions you report as incorrect.

Information created as you use Sarav

  • Practice activity: questions you answer, your scores, your streak, your mistakes deck and your league standings.
  • Subscription status: whether you have Sarav Pro or an exam pass, and when it renews or ends. Google Play handles payment, and we receive only the purchase record.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device model, Android version, app version, language setting and crash reports.
  • Usage information: which screens you open and which features you use, so we can improve the app.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show ads (see Ads).
  • Notification token: lets us send your daily reminder and exam alerts.

What we don't collect

We do not access your precise location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone. Your district is something you choose, not something we detect.

3. How we use your information

  • To run Sarav: your daily questions, streaks, mistake review and exam countdown.
  • To place you in weekly leagues with other aspirants from your district and exam.
  • To send reminders and notifications you've allowed, such as your daily practice reminder or recruitment alerts.
  • To manage subscriptions and exam passes.
  • To show ads to free users.
  • To fix bugs, improve questions and understand which features help, mostly using combined, non-identifying statistics.
  • To answer your support requests.
  • To prevent cheating in leagues, fraud and misuse, and to meet our legal obligations.

4. Consent

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account and use Sarav, and for the other lawful purposes the DPDP Act allows. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or writing to us. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything we did before, but Sarav can't work without the basic data it needs.

You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings at any time without deleting your account.

6. Ads

The free version of Sarav shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show more relevant ads. Learn more in Google's ad policy.

To limit personalised ads, open your phone's Settings → Google → Ads and delete or reset your advertising ID. Sarav Pro has no ads. Rewarded ads, such as watching an ad to earn a streak freeze, are always optional.

7. What other users can see

In weekly leagues, other aspirants can see your display name, district, league rank and weekly score. They cannot see your phone number, email or answers. You can change your display name at any time to stay anonymous.

8. How long we keep your information

  • We keep your account data while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days. Copies in encrypted backups are removed within a further 90 days.
  • We may keep purchase records for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • We may keep combined statistics that can't identify you, such as how many people answered a question correctly.
  • If you haven't used Sarav for [2 years], we may notify you and then delete your account.

9. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, access controls so each user can reach only their own records, and limited staff access. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access: get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct and update: fix inaccurate or incomplete data. Most of it you can edit in the app.
  • Erase: ask us to delete your data. Request deletion here.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if you're not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity.

To use any of these rights, write to hello@anveshlabs.com from the email or phone linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity. We resolve grievances within a reasonable time, and in any case within 90 days.

11. Age requirement

Sarav is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Where your data is stored

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Sarav grows or the law changes. If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
